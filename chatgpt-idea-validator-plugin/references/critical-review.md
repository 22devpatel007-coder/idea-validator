# Devil's Advocate

Try to disprove the idea:
1. Is the pain urgent enough to pay for?
2. Is the user also the buyer?
3. What is the strongest substitute?
4. Why would customers switch?
5. Why would incumbents not copy it?
6. Could acquisition cost exceed customer value?
7. Is the product dependent on a platform, API, or regulation?
8. Are there hidden operations/support costs?
9. Can this founder reach the market?
10. What evidence would make us stop?

For each risk state severity, evidence, fixability, and a resolving test. Be specific and fair.
