# Competitor Analysis

Include direct competitors, adjacent products, indirect alternatives, manual workarounds, internal tools, agencies, spreadsheets, messaging apps, offline processes, and doing nothing.

For each competitor capture product, segment, use case, verified price, positioning, strengths, evidence-backed weaknesses, switching costs, complaints, and source/date.

A meaningful differentiator must matter to a buyer, be understandable, and be hard enough to copy or neutralize. “More features” is not automatically differentiation.
