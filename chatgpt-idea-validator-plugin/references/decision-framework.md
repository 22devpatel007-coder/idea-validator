# Decision Framework

Score each category 0–5. Weighted points = rating / 5 × weight.

| Dimension | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Problem severity and frequency | 20 |
| Demand / willingness-to-pay evidence | 20 |
| Reachable market and timing | 10 |
| Differentiation | 10 |
| Business model and revenue potential | 10 |
| Customer acquisition feasibility | 10 |
| Technical and operational feasibility | 10 |
| Economics and sustainability | 10 |

Bands: 80–100 strong candidate; 65–79 focused test; 45–64 modify; 0–44 reject current form.

A score cannot override critical legal, safety, technical, or distribution blockers. If demand is unproven, do not give unconditional BUILD.

Confidence is separate: LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH, justified by source quality, recency, customer evidence, coverage, and missing data.

Verdicts:
- BUILD: evidence supports a narrow MVP.
- TEST: validate demand first.
- MODIFY: change segment, problem, offer, channel, or model.
- REJECT: current version is not worth pursuing.
Default to TEST if willingness to pay is unproven.
