# Financial Analysis

Model setup/development costs, recurring infrastructure, payment/support costs, price, gross margin, sales/marketing costs, CAC, churn/retention, and break-even.

Formulas:
- MRR = active paying subscribers × monthly subscription price.
- Gross profit = revenue − direct delivery costs.
- Gross margin = gross profit ÷ revenue.
- CAC = acquisition spend ÷ new paying customers.
- Break-even customers = fixed monthly costs ÷ contribution per customer per month.

Keep periods consistent. Create pessimistic, base, and optimistic cases. Label assumptions and illustrative values. Do not call revenue profit. Include taxes, founder compensation, refunds, churn, and acquisition costs where material or list exclusions.
