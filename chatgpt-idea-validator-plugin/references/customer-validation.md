# Customer Validation

Identify user, buyer, approver, and payer. Ask about actual past behavior:
- Tell me about the last time this problem happened.
- What did you do?
- How often does it happen?
- What did it cost?
- What have you tried?
- Who decides whether to buy?
- What prevents switching?

Avoid leading hypothetical questions such as “Would you use my app?”

Evidence ladder (context-dependent): positive reaction, stated interest, follow-up commitment, prototype use, workflow/data sharing, signed pilot, actual payment and repeated use.

Design tests with hypothesis, segment, recruitment, sample limitations, offer, metric, success/failure thresholds, and decision. Never fabricate interviews.
