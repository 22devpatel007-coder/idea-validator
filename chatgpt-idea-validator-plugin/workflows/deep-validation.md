# Deep Research

1. Frame idea and assumption register.
2. Define initial customer segment and geography.
3. Research market demand and customer pain.
4. Research competitors, substitutes, and pricing.
5. Investigate buying behavior and willingness-to-pay evidence.
6. Analyze business model, distribution, retention, and operations.
7. Assess technical feasibility, privacy, security, and regulation.
8. Build transparent financial scenarios.
9. Run Devil's Advocate.
10. Audit evidence quality, dates, geography, and contradictions.
11. Score viability and confidence separately.
12. Decide BUILD / TEST / MODIFY / REJECT.
13. Design one low-cost validation experiment.
14. Produce report and sources.

If live research is unavailable, do not simulate browsing. Give a preliminary assessment and research checklist.
