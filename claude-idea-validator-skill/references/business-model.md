# Business Model Guide

Investigate:
- Who is the user, the buyer, the approver, and the payer? Note where they differ.
- Revenue model options (subscription, usage, one-time, transaction fee, services-led) and which fits how value is delivered.
- Who captures the value, and how it compares with the price of the current workaround.
- Sales cycle and channel: self-serve, outbound, partnerships, marketplaces, communities. Estimate time to first revenue.
- Recurring versus one-time revenue, and the drivers of retention versus churn.
- Operational requirements: onboarding, support load, compliance, and any manual work hidden behind the product.
- Dependence on a platform, API, or partner that could change terms.

Output: the leading business model, one credible alternative, the riskiest model assumption, and how to test it cheaply.
