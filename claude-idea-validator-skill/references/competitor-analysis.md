# Competitor Analysis Guide

Investigate:
- direct competitors;
- indirect competitors;
- manual workarounds;
- internal tools customers already use;
- agencies, consultants, spreadsheets, messaging apps, or offline processes;
- doing nothing.

For each important competitor, record:
- product and target segment;
- core use case;
- pricing and billing basis, if verifiable;
- distribution and positioning;
- strengths;
- weaknesses supported by evidence;
- switching costs;
- customer complaints or unmet needs;
- source and date checked.

## Differentiation test

A useful difference should matter to a buyer, be easy to understand, and be hard enough to copy or neutralize.

Do not recommend “more features” as differentiation without explaining why buyers value them. A competitor matrix should compare outcomes and buyer segments, not only feature checklists.
