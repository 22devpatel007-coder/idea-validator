# Market Analysis Guide

Answer these questions:

1. Who has the problem? Define a narrow initial segment.
2. How often does the problem occur?
3. How painful or costly is it?
4. What happens if the customer does nothing?
5. What triggers the customer to seek a solution?
6. Who controls the budget?
7. How many plausible customers can the founder realistically reach?
8. What market or regulatory changes matter?

## Market sizing

Prefer bottom-up sizing:
- identify the target customer population;
- estimate the reachable subset;
- show the source and assumptions;
- estimate plausible adoption only when evidence supports it.

Separate:
- TAM: broad theoretical market;
- SAM: segment the product can serve;
- SOM: realistically obtainable share in an initial period.

Do not invent precise numbers. If reliable inputs are missing, provide a sizing framework and identify the missing data.
