# Critical Review / Devil's Advocate

Try to falsify the idea with concrete arguments.

Ask:
1. Is the problem painful enough to motivate a purchase?
2. Is the proposed buyer the person who experiences the problem?
3. What is the strongest substitute?
4. Why would a customer switch?
5. Why would an incumbent not copy the feature?
6. Is customer acquisition likely to cost more than the customer is worth?
7. Does the business depend on a platform, API, policy, or regulation it cannot control?
8. Are there hidden operational or support costs?
9. Is the proposed market reachable by this founder?
10. What evidence would make us stop?

For every criticism, state:
- severity (low / medium / high / critical);
- evidence;
- whether it is fixable;
- the test that could resolve it.

Do not invent objections just to sound skeptical. The goal is accurate risk discovery.
