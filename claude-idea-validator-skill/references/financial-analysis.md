# Financial Analysis Guide

## Minimum model

List:
- one-time setup/development costs;
- recurring infrastructure and software costs;
- payment processing and support costs;
- price and billing frequency;
- expected gross margin;
- sales and marketing costs;
- customer acquisition assumptions;
- churn or retention assumptions where relevant.

## Useful formulas

- Monthly recurring revenue (MRR) = active paying subscribers × monthly subscription price.
- Gross profit = revenue − direct cost of delivering the service.
- Gross margin = gross profit ÷ revenue.
- CAC = sales and marketing acquisition spend ÷ new paying customers acquired.
- Break-even customers = fixed monthly costs ÷ contribution per customer per month.

Use consistent periods and units. Do not compare monthly costs with annual revenue without converting.

## Scenarios

Create pessimistic, base, and optimistic cases. For each, show the assumptions that differ. Avoid false precision and explain which assumptions dominate the result.

Never call revenue “profit.” Include taxes, founder compensation, refunds, churn, and acquisition costs when material, or explicitly state that they are excluded.

If key inputs are unavailable, present a fill-in model instead of inventing a forecast.
