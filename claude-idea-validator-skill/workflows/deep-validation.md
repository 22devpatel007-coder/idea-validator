# Deep Validation Workflow

Use for deep-research requests or ideas with substantial cost, risk, or regulation. Output: `templates/final-decision.md`.

1. Frame the idea and build the assumption register.
2. Fix a narrow initial segment and geography.
3. Market and customer pain: read `references/market-analysis.md`.
4. Competitors, substitutes, and pricing: read `references/competitor-analysis.md`.
5. Buyers and willingness-to-pay evidence: read `references/customer-validation.md`.
6. Business model, distribution, retention, operations: read `references/business-model.md`.
7. Technical feasibility, dependencies, privacy, regulation: read `references/technical-feasibility.md`.
8. Financial scenarios: read `references/financial-analysis.md`.
9. Devil's advocate review: read `references/critical-review.md`.
10. Evidence audit: label claims, check dates and geography, resolve contradictions.
11. Score and set confidence: read `references/scoring-and-verdicts.md`.
12. Verdict, then one experiment with thresholds.
13. Full report with sources.

Without research tools, deliver a clearly marked preliminary assessment and a research checklist. Do not simulate browsing.
