# Compare Ideas Workflow

Use when the user wants to choose between two or more ideas (or variants of one idea).

1. Define each idea in one line: customer, problem, offer. Ask for the founder constraints once, since they apply to all ideas.
2. Research each idea at the **same depth**. Never compare a deeply researched idea against a lightly researched one without saying so.
3. Score every idea with the same rubric (`references/scoring-and-verdicts.md`), including demand caps. Record confidence per idea.
4. Build the comparison table below.
5. Rank on two axes: (a) viability score, and (b) how cheaply the idea can be tested. A lower-scoring idea that can be tested in a week may be the right first move.
6. Recommend one idea to test first, with one experiment. Say what result would make the runner-up the better choice.
7. If scores are within about 8 points, or confidence is Low, call it a tie and decide on testability and founder fit.
