# Quick Validation Workflow

Use for a fast screen. Output: `templates/quick-report.md`.

1. Clarify customer, problem, geography, and offer. Ask only for essential missing information (max three questions) or proceed on labeled assumptions.
2. List the three to five assumptions that matter most.
3. Check obvious direct competitors, substitutes, and the "do nothing" option with available search tools. Search distinct question groups (see `references/research-methodology.md`).
4. Rate the dimensions in `references/scoring-and-verdicts.md`, using the anchors. Thin source coverage means confidence is Low, so show a score range.
5. Run the devil's advocate review (`references/critical-review.md`), limited to the top three risks.
6. Give the verdict.
7. Recommend one cheap experiment with success and failure thresholds.
8. List what was not researched. Do not present a quick screen as due diligence, and offer deep research as the next step.
